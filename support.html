Frequently asked questions

💧How do I enable water tracking?

Open the menu and go to Settings. Under "Tracking" you can turn water tracking on or off and set your daily goal.

🎯How do I change my goals in the diary?

Tap the goal icon at the top of the diary. There you can customize your targets for calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, sugar, fiber, and salt.

🌐How do I switch the app language?

Open Settings and select Language under "Appearance". German and English are currently available.

👥My invite code for a cookbook isn't working

Invite codes are only valid for a limited time. Ask the cookbook's owner for a new code using the share button.

🔄My data isn't showing up on another device

Make sure you're signed in with the same account on both devices and have an internet connection. Syncing happens automatically in the background.

🗑️How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Profile > Delete account. All cookbooks, recipes, shopping lists, and diary entries will be permanently removed.

👑What's included in the fitomi Pro plan?

Pro unlocks additional statistics and removes limits on cookbooks and recipes, among other things. See the app under Profile for details.
